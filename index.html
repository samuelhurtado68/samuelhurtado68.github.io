From a new language to a new architecture

I moved to France in 2019 without speaking a word of French. Three years later I passed my Baccalauréat with honors — proof, if nothing else, that I don't mind being dropped into an unfamiliar system and figuring out how it works.

That's more or less how I approach electronics too. I've always taken things apart to see what's inside, and that curiosity is what got me building a RISC-V CPU emulator in my spare time, and picking apart FPGAs and microcontrollers in my coursework.

At Polytech Nantes I'm studying electronics and embedded systems, and I'm now looking for a team abroad to keep learning with — ideally somewhere close to microprocessor architecture.
